# Design documentation

How Make The Team is organised, how it looks, and the rules a screen is held to. Current as of
23 September 2026 (after M66).

- **[Information architecture](information-architecture.md)** — audiences, navigation, a site
  map, and every screen: who reaches it, what is on it, and why.
- **[Design system](design-system.md)** — tokens, type, components, and how styling is wired
  through the CSP.
- **[UI standards](ui-standards.md)** — product constraints, design rules, a review checklist,
  and the known places the app does not meet them yet.

The product guide for players and organisers is [`docs/guide/`](../guide/README.md); its
screenshots illustrate these pages too.

## Keeping this current

These pages describe the shipped app. A change that adds a page, changes who can reach one,
adds a token or a component, or breaks a rule here updates the page in the same commit. The
checklist in [UI standards](ui-standards.md#3-review-checklist) says so.

Earlier documents these replace — the `screens.md` inventory (to M52) and the September design
review — are kept in [`docs/history/`](../history/) for their reasoning. Milestone design specs
in `docs/superpowers/specs/` record decisions at the time they were made and are not updated
afterwards.
